# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

WordPress with Elementor (free version). The deliverable from this phase is static HTML prototypes of every page, built to match the approved landing page `home.html`; no Elementor build notes were requested.

## Users

From FLI's own RFQ and vendor brief:

1. Funders and donors checking credibility, results and governance.
2. Language communities: speakers, writers, poets, teachers and community-based organisations (CBOs) in northern Pakistan, often on phones and slow connections, many reading Urdu.
3. Researchers and linguists looking for publications, papers and language data.
4. Educators and provincial education departments (mother-tongue education).
5. Partners and media.

## Product Purpose

fli-online.org is the public home of the Forum for Language Initiatives (FLI), a non-profit registered in Islamabad (CRRFA/ICT/27, est. 2002, Executive Director Fakhruddin Akhunzada). FLI helps the minority and endangered language communities of northern Pakistan document, develop and sustain their languages. The site must:

1. show credibility and impact to funders (annual reports, results, governance);
2. give communities and researchers the resources (books, fonts, keyboards, papers);
3. report news and events regularly;
4. grow the email subscriber list;
5. be an authoritative reference on the languages of northern Pakistan.

## Positioning

FLI works with about 30 languages of Chitral, Gilgit-Baltistan, Swat, Kohistan, Dir, Hazara, Azad Kashmir and South Waziristan, and its model puts "mother tongue speakers in the driving seat": community researchers trained by FLI write the orthographies, books and keyboards themselves.

## Operating Context

- Programmes: writers' and orthography workshops, language documentation training, vitality surveys, MLE (mother-tongue-based multilingual education) and curriculum work, publishing, digital tools (fonts, Android and PC keyboards), partner networks of CBOs, cultural festivals and mushairas, and academic conferences (IHCC4 2022, FEL XXVIII 2024).
- Content volume on the current site: 217 news posts (2013–2026, 2–4 a month in 2026), about 80 books in three series, newsletters 2012–2026, about 30 language profiles.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Site sections (vendor brief, confirmed 22 Oct 2026): Home, About FLI, Programs / Our Work, Languages and Communities, Research and Publications, News and Events, Media and Resources, Get Involved, Contact.
- Content types: Publications, Resources, News, Events, Languages, Programmes, Team.
- Requirements: mobile-first (360/768/1024/1440), touch targets of at least 44 px, menu of at most 3 levels, prominent global search, faceted publication filters, CTAs for donate, partner, volunteer and subscribe, footer with privacy, terms, address and a working email.
- Performance targets: mobile PageSpeed 85+, LCP of 2.5 s or less; no blocking preloader.
- Donations: no online payment; the donate button links to FLI's existing method (none is published yet).
- Out of scope: copywriting and translation.

## Brand Commitments

- Name "Forum for Language Initiatives (FLI)"; existing logo (`/site/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/for-web-300x169.png`).
- The approved prototype `home.html` sets the visual system for every page.
- Brand line: "Preserving the wealth of regional cultures and languages of Pakistan."

## Evidence on Hand

- Achievements (Success Stories page): 23 CBOs established; 15 PC and 15 Android keyboards; orthography issues resolved for 11 communities, with new orthographies for 7 languages; documentation training for people from 26 communities; 72 books published or supported in 17 languages; mother-tongue preschool programmes in 6 communities; 2 international conferences.
- Testimonials (Impact Stories pages, February 2025, plus Dawn 2 Dec 2024 and Sunday Express 10 Nov 2025 reviews).
- Board bios for 7 members, with photos for 3 (Fouzia Qazi, Dr. Farhat Abdullah, Fazal Karim Khan).
- Absences that must not be invented: no street address (P.O. Box 763 only), no donation method, no privacy policy or terms, no ISBNs, and no speaker figures beyond those on the profiles. The homepage stat "24/7 support" is not supported by any FLI source.

## Product Principles

1. Communities first: show the people and languages doing the work, not the organisation talking about itself.
2. Every claim dated or sourced; no unsupported numbers.
3. Resources are one tap away: books, keyboards and fonts should never be more than two clicks from any page.
4. Built for the slowest phone in Chitral: light pages and readable type, in Urdu as well as English.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA on core templates. Urdu content is marked up with `lang="ur" dir="rtl"` and set in a Nastaliq font that covers the extended letters of northern Pakistan's languages.
