Board members

Chairman

Rozi Khan Burki

Linguist, poet, author and former Member of the Federal Board of Revenue; a native Ormuri speaker and the language's leading advocate.

Rozi Khan Burki is a distinguished linguist, poet, author and former senior civil servant who retired from the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) as a Member. Throughout his career he combined public service with a deep commitment to the preservation and promotion of Pakistan's linguistic and cultural heritage.

Read more

A native speaker of Ormuri, an endangered language spoken by a small community in South Waziristan, he has played a pioneering role in safeguarding and revitalising his mother tongue through literature, educational resources and community engagement.

As a poet and author he has produced valuable publications in Ormuri, including the Ormuri Dictionary, and has developed reading and learning materials that support literacy among younger speakers. He is recognised as one of the leading champions of Ormuri language preservation.

Board member

Fouzia Qazi

Development professional with 26+ years in project management, gender initiatives and entrepreneurship.

Read full biography

Fouzia Qazi is a seasoned development professional with more than 26 years of expertise in project management, gender initiatives and entrepreneurship. She holds an MSc in Applied Development Studies from the University of Reading, UK, as a Joint Japan/World Bank Graduate Scholar, and a BSc in Agriculture from the Agriculture University, Peshawar.

She has a strong background in grassroots mobilisation, women's empowerment and natural resource management, with organisations including AKRSP, USAID, World Vision International, Hashoo Foundation and the Women Development Directorate (GB). She founded Rove Pakistan Travel & Tours and has been an active member of the Rawalpindi and Hunza Chambers of Commerce.

Board member

Dr. Farhat Abdullah

Linguist and faculty member at the University of Central Punjab, Lahore, certified in language documentation.

Read full biography

Dr. Farhat Abdullah has an active career in teaching and research spanning over a decade. In 2018 the Higher Education Commission awarded her a Faculty Development Program Scholarship for her PhD, and she held a research fellowship at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore.

In 2016 she attended CoLang, the Linguistic Society of America's Collaborative Language Research institute in Fairbanks, Alaska, earning certification in language documentation. She contributed to the DAAD-funded Urdu WordNet project and has taught linguistics at the University of Central Punjab, Lahore, since 2013, winning its Best Faculty Teacher Award in 2021.

Board member

Razwal Kohistani

Linguist, author and cultural researcher from Indus Kohistan; four decades documenting Kohistani Shina.

Read full biography

Razwal Kohistani is a renowned linguist, author and cultural researcher from Indus Kohistan, celebrated for over four decades of pioneering work documenting and standardising Kohistani Shina. With an MBA in Finance and a career that included WWF Pakistan, he created a three-volume Shina–Kohistani–Urdu dictionary and developed an Arabic-based script and digital keyboard for Kohistani Shina.

He received the Presidential Award in 2024 for a Shina-language biography of the Prophet Muhammad, and has authored or co-authored nearly 50 works in Shina, Urdu and English, often with international scholars, on phonology, grammar, sociolinguistics, oral traditions and the history of Kohistan.

Board member

Fazal Karim Khan

Monitoring and evaluation specialist with 18+ years on multisector donor-funded projects across Pakistan.

Read full biography

Fazal Karim Khan has more than 18 years of experience in the design, implementation and monitoring and evaluation of multisector projects in civic education, livelihoods, education, governance and emergency response, including in the Newly Merged Districts and Balochistan.

He has worked on projects funded by USAID, the EU, the World Bank, FCDO, IFAD and CBM, with organisations including DAI, Save the Children, Blumont and OPM. He holds master's degrees in social sciences and English literature and speaks English, Urdu and Pashto.

Board member

Zulfiqar Khan

Former Director Curriculum and Teacher Education, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa; 31 years in education.

Read full biography

With a career spanning over 31 years, Zulfiqar Khan retired as Director Curriculum and Teacher Education, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. He has contributed extensively to curriculum development from ECCE to Grade XII and ALP I–VIII, designed schemes of studies for formal and non-formal education, and led the development and review of textbooks and supplementary reading materials.

His expertise extends to assessment tools, training manuals and modules, and he has served as master trainer, administrator, coordinator and planner of educational training.
