Start work on your language.

If your community wants to write, publish or teach in its mother tongue, FLI can help. This is the path most communities have taken with us.

  1. Get in touch. Tell us about your language, where it is spoken and who is ready to work on it.
  2. Issues in Language Development workshop. Together we find what the language needs first.
  3. Orthography workshop. Your community chooses how the language will be written.
  4. Writers' workshop. Poets, teachers and students start writing.
  5. First books and a keyboard. FLI prints community books and builds a keyboard layout.
