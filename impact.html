The record so far.

From FLI's achievements record, updated January 2026.

72

Published, or helped publish, 72 books in and about 17 indigenous languages and cultures of northern Pakistan.

Browse the library
23

Motivated 23 communities to set up community-based organisations, giving language work an organised home in each one.

Our partner network
15+15

Developed 15 PC and 15 Android keyboards, so mother tongues can be typed on phones, computers and social media.

Get a keyboard
7

Developed new orthographies for 7 languages of northern Pakistan, and helped 11 communities resolve orthography issues.

How we do it
26

Trained people from 26 indigenous language communities in language documentation.

Training
6

Supported a mother-tongue-based preschool programme in six indigenous language communities.

Mother-tongue education
2

Partnered in two international academic conferences on indigenous languages: IHCC4 in 2022 and FEL XXVIII in 2024.

Conferences

And beyond the numbers

  • Raised awareness among indigenous communities of the importance of mother tongues.
  • Trained government and private school teachers to teach the newly introduced mother-tongue subjects in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
  • Contributed to the recognition of indigenous languages at universities, supporting documentation work and their inclusion in courses.
  • Resolved inconsistencies in KP government textbooks for Pashto, Hindko, Saraiki and Khowar by training book reviewers.
  • Connected local researchers to collaborate on lesser-known languages through workshops, seminars and conferences.
  • Raised COVID-19 awareness in 15 language communities with messages in their mother tongues.
