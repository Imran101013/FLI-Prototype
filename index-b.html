What FLI does.

From writers' workshops to fonts and keyboards, our programs and services support language development and cultural documentation.

Training

Equipping mother tongue speakers with the skills to lead language development in their own communities.

  • Discovery Course
  • MLE Committee Training
  • Curriculum & Materials Development

Publications

Newsletters, books, academic papers, translations and annual reports in and about local languages.

  • Vernacular Series
  • Language & Culture Series
  • Newsletters
  • Translated materials

Digital resources

Free fonts, keyboards, software and research tools, so mother tongues can be used on digital devices and social media.

  • Fonts
  • Keyboards
  • Software and research tools

Advocacy & networking

Raising awareness and connecting communities with scholars and partners at home and abroad.

  • Partners meetings

Our vision is to see all ethnolinguistic communities in Pakistan flourishing culturally and economically.

The record so far, from FLI's achievements record, updated January 2026.

72 books in 17 languages

Published, or helped publish, 72 books in and about 17 indigenous languages and cultures of northern Pakistan.

23 community organisations

Motivated 23 communities to set up community-based organisations, to provide an organised platform for language development work within their communities.

15 PC + 15 Android keyboards

Developed 15 PC and 15 Android keyboards, so mother tongues can be typed on phones, computers and social media.

7 new orthographies

Developed new orthographies for 7 languages of northern Pakistan, and helped 11 communities resolve orthography issues.

26 communities trained

Trained people from 26 indigenous language communities in language documentation.

Preschool in 6 communities

Supported a mother-tongue-based preschool programme in six indigenous language communities.

2 international conferences

Partnered in organising two international academic conferences: the 4th International Hindukush Cultural Conference (2022) and the 28th FEL Conference (2024).

Established 2002 and registered with the Islamabad Capital Territory administration, No. CRRFA/ICT/27.
