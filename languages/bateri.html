Bateri بٹیری

Belonging to the Kohistani group, the Bateri language is spoken in Batera, which is in the southern region of the Kohistan district along the east bank of the Indus River.

According to the Population Census the Federal Government of Pakistan conducted in 2017, the total recorded number of people in Batera Union Council was 18,000. Since Batera UC is also populated by people other than Bateri speakers, the number of Bateri speakers is estimated to be up to 15,000.

This figure comes from Bateri researcher Muhammad Baqi, who has long collected data about his native language and has recently begun new research with an international researcher. Some speakers of Bateri are also found in India; more than 2,000 were reported there in 2010, though no updated data is available.

So far the language lacks a functional writing system. FLI started its documentation services for Bateri in 2021 with a language vitality survey, which found the language vulnerable in all three domains: identity, orality and literacy. Activities planned on the basis of this survey are expected to lead to proper documentation of the language.
