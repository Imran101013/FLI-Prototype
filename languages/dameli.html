Dameli دمیلی

Dameli is spoken in several isolated villages in a side valley called Damel, on the east side of the Chitral River, a few miles below Mirkhani in Arandu Tehsil, Chitral.

It has been substantially influenced by neighbouring languages and is threatened by larger surrounding language communities as well as Urdu. Yet Dameli speakers still use the language at home, with family and friends, in community and religious gatherings, and informally in schools.

The Dameli people are mostly farmers and goat herders. Many young people migrate to the cities for work, especially in the food industry in Peshawar and Karachi. Research around 2010 estimated 5,000 Dameli speakers; Asmat Dameli, a researcher who took part in FLI's intervention, estimates the number could now be around 6,000.

The language has a functional writing system, developed by trained Dameli speakers in 2016 under a preliminary documentation project implemented by FLI. These researchers have since published five books on various topics, recorded their folktales, translated them into widely known languages, and are promoting their mother tongue on social media.
