Palula پالولا

Whether you take the old winding road over the Lowari Pass or drive through the new tunnel, the first communities you meet entering Chitral by road are the Palula.

Get the keyboard 12 books in the library
About 15,000Speakers
ChitralRegion
Shina groupIndo-Aryan family

They live in the Ashret and Biori valleys and are by tradition considered the gatekeepers of Chitral. Palula is related to Shina and is widely used between Lowari and the small town of Drosh.

In recent years a written form of the language has been developed and is used in a small but growing body of literature and educational materials. FLI ran its flagship multilingual education programme in Palula, followed by an adult literacy project, and has published Palula alphabet, primer, Grade 1 and Grade 2 books and a picture dictionary.

Most people farm on small plots; land is scarce, so many migrate for seasonal or permanent work. The community has a rich heritage of oral history, traditions and sayings. In 2026, 23 Palula poets learned to write in their language at an FLI workshop, and FLI signed an MoU with the Palula language community.
