Torwali توروالی

Torwali, a Dardic language of the Indo-Aryan family, is spoken in the Bahrain and Chail areas of District Swat.

By some estimates the Torwali people number between 120,000 and 130,000. Bahrain is the cultural and administrative centre of the language and probably home to about 70 percent of the speakers living in the Swat valley.

Rigorous work on revitalising Torwali has been under way since 2004. Torwali speakers are very proud of their language and culture. Many men and a growing number of women also speak Urdu and Pashto, but they use Torwali with one another, though increasingly mixed with Urdu, Pashto and English words. FLI has published Torwali alphabet, primer, Grade 1 and Grade 2 books and a picture dictionary.

Traditionally most Torwalis were subsistence farmers; today they grow mostly corn, wheat, tomatoes, apples and pears for local markets and the cities to the south.
