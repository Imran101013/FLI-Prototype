Orthography & documentation

Work on a new language begins with an Issues in Language Development (ILD) workshop, which identifies the problems that may stand in the way of documenting it; Kalkoti's in June 2021 had 13 community participants, Mankiyali's in 2019 had 16. An orthography workshop follows, where participants choose how their language will be written.

FLI has helped 11 communities resolve orthography issues and developed new orthographies for seven languages. People from 26 language communities have been trained in documentation, including at FLI's Language Documentation Workshop on tools and technology in Islamabad in November 2021.

Mother-tongue education (MLE)

FLI's mother-tongue-based multilingual education work trains MLE committees, supports preschool programmes in six language communities, and builds the capacity of government and private school teachers to teach the mother-tongue subjects newly introduced in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Curriculum & materials development

Language and Materials Development (LMD) workshops produce the primers, readers and picture dictionaries in FLI's library. FLI has also trained KP textbook reviewers to resolve inconsistencies in the Pashto, Hindko, Saraiki and Khowar textbooks, and in January 2026 trained Gojri curriculum developers in Muzaffarabad.
